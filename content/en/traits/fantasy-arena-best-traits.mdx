## Understanding How Traits Work in Fantasy Arena

Traits function as passive modifiers and active abilities that define your character's power curve across a run. Unlike classes—which you purchase from the class shop—traits are equipped from the trait shop and can be swapped between runs.

### Trait Categories Explained

| Trait Category | Function | Impact Level |
|----------------|----------|--------------|
| Offensive | Boost damage, attack speed, crit chance | High |
| Defensive | Increase HP, armor, damage reduction | Medium-High |
| Utility | Enhance currency gain, tower range, cooldowns | Medium |
| Summoning | Spawn minions, turrets, or pylons | Very High (Meta) |

Each category serves a distinct purpose. However, based on community reports, summoning-style traits currently outperform others by a significant margin—especially when paired with the right class.

## The Mage Class and Its Dominant Pylon Trait

Player experience consistently highlights the Mage class as one of the most powerful options in the current meta. The reason? Its signature pylon tower trait.

The Mage's pylon tower spawns autonomous turrets that attack enemies within range. According to community gameplay, these towers feature exceptional range for a class at this rarity tier. Players report that enemies frequently get stuck on map ramps, and strategically placed pylons can essentially spawn-kill incoming waves.

### Why the Pylon Tower Trait Dominates

The key insight from experienced players: turn off auto-skip. When auto-skip is disabled, enemies linger longer at spawn points, giving your towers more time to eliminate them before they advance.

## How to Build Around the Fantasy Arena Best Traits

Trait selection is only half the equation. How you build and play around those traits determines your actual results.

### Step-by-Step Pylon Tower Strategy

1. **Earn starting burgers** – Redeem active codes from the codes board before entering a match
2. **Equip the Mage class** – Save up and purchase directly rather than rolling; this preserves your class slot
3. **Enter a match and save currency** – Resist the urge to place towers immediately
4. **Identify the ramp chokepoint** – Enemies path unnaturally near map ramps, creating kill zones
5. **Place 3-4 pylons near the ramp** – Maximize overlap coverage

8. **Disable auto-skip** – Keep enemies at spawn longer for maximum tower uptime
