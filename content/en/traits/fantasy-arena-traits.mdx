## What Are Fantasy Arena Traits?

Fantasy Arena is a zombie arena survival game where you fend off increasingly difficult waves of enemies using magical wands. The recent update introduced a trait system that attaches passive abilities to wands rated legendary or higher. According to community reports, some of these traits are inspired by another popular Roblox title, Dead Spells, which recently overhauled its own trait system with 14 reworks and 8 brand-new additions.

In Fantasy Arena, traits function as passive buffs that enhance your wand's performance in combat. Unlike active skills that require mana or cooldowns, traits are always on—working silently in the background to boost your damage, survivability, or utility.

### How Traits Differ from Regular Buffs

| Feature | Regular Buffs | Traits |
|---|---|---|
| Activation | Requires item or skill | Always passive |
| Duration | Time-limited | Permanent while equipped |
| Equipment Requirement | Any gear | Legendary+ wands only |
| Stacking | Often limited | Can combine with buffs |
| Customization | Minimal | Build-defining |

This distinction matters because traits fundamentally change how you approach loadouts. Instead of relying solely on potion buffs or temporary power-ups, your wand's trait becomes a core part of your strategy.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are Fantasy Arena traits and how do they work?
Fantasy Arena traits are passive abilities attached to wands of legendary rarity or higher. They provide automatic buffs—such as increased damage, defense, or utility—without requiring activation. Every trait is always active while the wand is equipped, making them a core part of your build strategy.

### Can I get traits on non-legendary wands?
No. Currently, Fantasy Arena traits are exclusive to legendary and mythic-tier wands. Common, uncommon, rare, and epic wands do not have trait slots. You'll need to progress through the game and obtain higher-rarity wands to access the trait system.

### Will more traits be added to Fantasy Arena in future updates?
Yes. The developer has indicated that more content is coming, including harder difficulties and additional traits. Based on how the trait system evolved in the related game Dead Spells—which went from basic stat buffs to complex passive mechanics—players can expect Fantasy Arena traits to become significantly deeper over time.
