## Fantasy Arena Boss Build Overview

A Fantasy Arena Boss Build must survive longer fights while still dealing enough damage to avoid being overwhelmed by the next wave. Boss builds are different from farming builds because they cannot rely only on early economy or fast clear.

For encounter context, read [Fantasy Arena Bosses](/en/bosses). For event-specific pressure, read the [Magma Event](/en/bosses/fantasy-arena-magma-event) guide.

## Boss Build Core

| Slot | Best Direction | Reason |
|------|----------------|--------|
| Class | Sustained damage or safer pressure | Bosses punish builds that only burst once. |
| Wand | Consistent boss damage | Single-target or steady area damage helps. |
| Traits | Damage plus survival | Boss pressure needs both output and safety. |
| Codes | Claim before upgrading | Free rewards can fund the missing piece. |

## Boss Build Priorities

1. Claim [Fantasy Arena Codes](/en/codes/fantasy-arena-codes).
2. Choose a class that can keep contributing during long fights.
3. Equip a wand that does not fall off against tougher targets.
4. Add one survival trait if you die before dealing enough damage.
5. Add damage or cooldown if the boss lives too long.

## Common Boss Build Mistakes

- Bringing a pure farming build into boss pressure.
- Ignoring survival because early waves felt easy.
- Choosing a wand only by rarity instead of boss usefulness.
- Spending resources before checking active codes.
- Forgetting that boss builds still need regular wave clear.

## Fantasy Arena Boss Build FAQ

### What is the best build for Fantasy Arena bosses?

The best boss build balances sustained damage, survival, and wand consistency. Start with the [Bosses](/en/bosses) hub, then compare classes, wands, and traits.

### Are boss builds good for farming?

Not always. Boss builds are safer but may be slower than farming-focused builds. Use [Best Builds](/en/builds/fantasy-arena-best-builds) to choose by goal.

### Should I use different traits for bosses?

Yes. If bosses are the wall, damage, cooldown, and survivability traits usually matter more than pure economy.
