## Fantasy Arena Solo Build Overview

A Fantasy Arena solo build needs consistency more than flash. Without teammates, your build must clear waves, survive mistakes, and handle boss pressure without depending on someone else's damage or support.

Start by claiming [Fantasy Arena Codes](/en/codes/fantasy-arena-codes), then spend rewards on the weakest part of your class, wand, and trait setup.

## Solo Build Priorities

| Priority | What to Build | Why It Matters Solo |
|----------|---------------|---------------------|
| 1 | Reliable wave clear | You cannot let enemies stack up. |
| 2 | Survivability | One mistake can end the run. |
| 3 | Wand consistency | Solo play needs damage that works every wave. |
| 4 | Trait synergy | Traits should support the class and wand together. |
| 5 | Boss pressure | Bosses expose weak single-target damage. |

## Recommended Solo Route

1. Choose a simple class direction from [Fantasy Arena Classes](/en/classes).
2. Equip a wand that clears regular waves consistently.
3. Add one damage or uptime trait.
4. Add survivability before Hard Mode or boss pushes.
5. Compare the finished setup against [Fantasy Arena Best Builds](/en/builds/fantasy-arena-best-builds).

## Solo Build Mistakes

- Building only for damage and dying to avoidable pressure.
- Using economy traits too long after combat becomes the problem.
- Ignoring boss pressure until the run is already stuck.
- Spending code rewards without checking the current [Tier List](/en/tier).

## Solo Build FAQ

### What is the best Fantasy Arena solo build?

The best solo build is usually a balanced setup with reliable wave clear, one clear survival layer, and traits that support your class and wand. Mage-style builds are a good starting point for many players.

### Should solo players use Hard Mode?

Use Hard Mode only after your build can clear normal waves consistently. Read [Fantasy Arena Hard Mode](/en/guide/fantasy-arena-hard-mode) before switching.

### What should I upgrade first for solo play?

Upgrade the part that is causing losses. If enemies reach you, improve damage or uptime. If bosses kill you, add survival or boss pressure.
