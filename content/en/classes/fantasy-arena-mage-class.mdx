## Why the Mage Is the Best Starter Class in Fantasy Arena

If you want to stack burgers fast and push deep into endless waves without top-tier gear, the Fantasy Arena mage class is your answer. This turret-focused class turns a simple mechanic—placing Pylons—into one of the most efficient farming strategies available right now. Community reports suggest the Mage can even rival some mythic-tier classes in raw output, which is remarkable for a lower-rarity pick.

The secret lies in the Mage's Pylon towers. These turrets have exceptional range and damage for their cost, allowing you to sit back while they shred entire waves. Whether you are a brand-new player or a veteran looking for an optimized burger farm, understanding the Fantasy Arena mage class will dramatically accelerate your progress.

## The Wave 26 Reset Loop: Step-by-Step Burger Farming

This is the strategy that makes the Fantasy Arena mage class shine. The idea is simple: push to Wave 26, reset your character to bank the payout, and immediately start again. Each loop takes roughly four minutes and yields 125 to 150 burgers. Over 40 minutes, that compounds to approximately 1,500 burgers.

### Wave-by-Wave Breakdown

| Wave Range | Action | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Position on grassy platform, save resources | Get into position early |
| 4 | Place two Pylons near the ramp | Turrets handle early clearing |
| 5–7 | Upgrade both Pylons to level 3 | Prevent enemies from leaking |
| 8–12 | Upgrade Mage level and Polished Wand to level 4 | Boost personal damage output |
| 13–16 | Push Polished Wand to level 5, bring Pylons to level 4 | Cover anything turrets miss |
| 17–24 | Maintain and repair Pylons via upgrades | Upgrades restore full Pylon health |
| 25 | Top up Pylon health, drop extra Pylons for boss | Overbuild defenses—this is the hardest wave |
| 26 | Reset your character | Banks the payout and restarts the loop |

### Key Positioning Tips

Stand on the grassy platform throughout the run. This spot keeps you safe while your Pylons do the heavy lifting. When placing turrets, focus them near the ramp area—community reports indicate enemies tend to get stuck there, giving your towers more time to deal damage. As waves progress, keep adding Pylons to this choke point. With enough upgraded towers, you will essentially spawn-kill enemies before they can advance.

### Why Reset at Wave 26?

The burger payout scales well up to Wave 26, but beyond that the difficulty spike requires significantly more investment in upgrades and time. Resetting at 26 maximizes your burgers-per-minute ratio. You avoid the diminishing returns of later waves while keeping each loop tight and predictable.

## Advanced Mage Strategies for Pushing Higher Waves

The Wave 26 loop is perfect for farming, but what if you want to survive insane waves and see how far the Fantasy Arena mage class can really go? With a few adjustments, you can push far beyond the farming breakpoint.

### Turn Off Auto Skip for Endless Mode

When farming burgers, Auto Skip speeds things up. But for pushing your personal best wave count, turn it off. Without Auto Skip, you have time between waves to earn currency, reposition, and place additional Pylons. This breathing room is essential for surviving later waves where enemies hit much harder.

### Co-op Pylon Stacking

If you have friends who also play the Mage, coordinate your Pylon placements. Multiple Mages stacking turrets in the same choke point creates a kill zone that can handle virtually anything. The key is making sure everyone upgrades their towers to max level—under-leveled Pylons will fall quickly in deep waves.

### Ramp Exploit Strategy

Player experience suggests that enemies frequently get stuck on the ramp near the map's center. Concentrating your Pylons here creates a death trap where mobs funnel in and melt before they can move. This is especially effective in co-op, where overlapping turret ranges can cover the entire ramp from multiple angles.

### Upgrade as Repair

One often-overlooked mechanic: upgrading a damaged Pylon restores it to full health. Instead of letting a turret die and replacing it, use the upgrade as a heal. This is more cost-effective and keeps your defensive line intact during intense waves.

## Common Mistakes and Troubleshooting

Even with a straightforward strategy, small errors can derail your runs. Here are the most frequent issues players encounter and how to fix them.

- **Placing Pylons too late**: Have your first two down by Wave 4 at the latest. Late placement means enemies leak through before your defenses are established.
- **Skipping Pylon repairs**: When turrets take damage, upgrade them instead of ignoring it. A destroyed Pylon wastes all the currency you invested.
- **Neglecting wand upgrades**: Your Polished Wand needs to reach level 5 by mid-game to cover targets that slip past your turrets.
- **Wrong positioning**: Stay on the grassy platform. Wandering around the map wastes time and exposes you to unnecessary damage.
- **Forgetting to reset at Wave 26**: Going past 26 without a strong enough build eats into your burgers-per-minute efficiency.

If your runs are falling apart early, revisit the wave-by-wave table above and make sure you are hitting each milestone on time.
