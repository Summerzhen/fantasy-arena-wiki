## What Are Mythical Classes and How Do They Work?

Mythical classes sit at the top of Fantasy Arena's rarity hierarchy. Unlike common or rare classes, these elite options come with unique abilities, enhanced stats, and synergies that can completely shift the tide of battle. According to the game's official description, players can combine classes, wands, and traits to create devastating builds—and mythical classes serve as the foundation for the most powerful combinations.

The class system operates on a gacha-style roll mechanic. You can spend in-game currency (burgers) at the class shop to roll for random classes, or you can save up and purchase specific ones outright to lock in a class slot. Community reports suggest that saving up and buying directly is often more efficient than gambling on rolls, especially for high-value targets like a mythical class.

## Advanced Tips for Pushing Higher Waves

Once you've secured your **Fantasy Arena mythical class** and built a solid loadout, these advanced strategies will help you push further than ever before.

### Map Awareness and Positioning

Fantasy Arena's environments transform throughout the battle, which means your positioning strategy needs to adapt. Key considerations:

- **Learn the ramp choke points.** Enemies frequently get stuck on elevated terrain, creating natural kill zones.
- **Watch for environmental hazards.** The Magma event introduces lava zones that can damage both you and enemies—use them to your advantage.
- **Reposition towers between waves.** As the map changes, yesterday's optimal tower placement may be tomorrow's dead zone.

### Co-op Coordination

With a server size of 25 players, Fantasy Arena is built for cooperative play. Here's how to maximize team effectiveness:

- **Diversify class roles.** A team of four Mages is strong, but mixing in a mythical class with frontline presence creates a more balanced defense.
- **Communicate tower placement.** Overlapping tower zones waste resources—coordinate with teammates to cover different angles.
- **Share burger economy.** If one player is close to upgrading a tower to max level, prioritize feeding them resources for the biggest impact.

### Boss Encounter Preparation

Special enemies and extremely rare bosses appear at higher waves, and they require a different approach than standard enemies:

- **Save burst abilities** for boss spawns rather than wasting them on trash mobs
- **Upgrade towers to max level** before the expected boss wave
- **Keep the Phoenix Wand equipped** if available—reviving a teammate during a boss fight can prevent a full team wipe
