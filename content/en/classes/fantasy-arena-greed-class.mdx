Once you are in a match, the strategy unfolds in phases:

- **Early Phase**: Save your burgers without placing any towers. Patience here pays off exponentially later.
- **Mid Phase**: Place your first batch of pylon towers on the ramp area where enemies bottleneck. This location is critical—enemies get stuck on the ramp, extending the time your towers have to deal damage.

- **Endgame**: Upgrade all existing towers to maximum level. Fully upgraded towers can essentially spawn-kill enemies before they advance.

The Fantasy Arena greed class approach thrives on compounding investment. Every tower you place generates more burgers, which funds more towers, which generates even more burgers.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

Even with a strong strategy, certain mistakes can severely limit your results. Here are the most frequent errors players make:

- **Placing towers too early**: Spending burgers on towers before you have enough saved up slows your overall economy. Wait until you can place multiple towers at once.
- **Ignoring codes**: Free burgers from the codes board are essentially free towers. Always redeem available codes before starting a run.
- **Leaving auto skip on**: This cannot be stressed enough. Auto skip drastically reduces your farming efficiency.
- **Poor tower placement**: Placing towers away from the ramp reduces their effectiveness. The bottleneck is the key to the entire strategy.
- **Not upgrading towers**: Low-level towers cannot keep up with later waves. Prioritize upgrades to maintain your farming momentum.

The Fantasy Arena greed class method is forgiving, but these mistakes can turn a profitable run into a wasted effort.
