## Fantasy Arena Hard Mode Overview

Hard Mode is where Fantasy Arena starts punishing loose builds. A setup that clears normal waves can still fail if it has no plan for survivability, boss pressure, or late-wave scaling.

This guide keeps the preparation simple: claim current codes, choose a class direction, pair it with a wand, add traits that solve a real weakness, and use builds that can handle stronger waves.

## What Changes in Hard Mode

Hard Mode should be treated as a build check. Even if exact numbers shift between updates, players should expect tougher enemies, less room for mistakes, and more pressure on weak class and wand pairings.

| Pressure Point | What It Tests | How to Prepare |
|----------------|---------------|----------------|
| Early waves | Whether your wand can clear safely | Use a reliable damage wand and avoid over-greedy upgrades. |
| Mid waves | Whether your build has enough consistency | Add traits that improve uptime, safety, or damage. |
| Boss encounters | Whether you can handle focused pressure | Read boss guides and keep your build goal clear. |
| Longer runs | Whether your class and wand still scale | Compare with tier and build pages before investing. |

## Wand and Trait Priorities

In Hard Mode, do not pick a wand only because it is rare. Pick a wand that answers your run's biggest problem.

| Problem | Wand Priority | Trait Priority |
|---------|---------------|----------------|
| Enemies reach you too fast | Reliable area pressure or safe range | Damage uptime or movement safety |
| Bosses take too long | Burst windows or steady single-target value | Damage scaling or survivability |
| You die before scaling | Sustain, recovery, or safer positioning | Health, defense, or consistency |
| Rewards feel slow | Efficient wave clear | Economy or farming support when available |

## Hard Mode Mistakes to Avoid

- Entering Hard Mode before redeeming active codes.
- Using a class, wand, and traits that all solve different problems.
- Spending resources on rolls when your current class choice is the real issue.
- Ignoring boss pages until after your build starts failing.
- Assuming a normal-mode setup will scale without changes.

## Hard Mode FAQ

### When should I try Fantasy Arena Hard Mode?

Try Hard Mode once your normal runs feel stable and your build has a clear class, wand, and trait plan. If you are still unsure what to upgrade, read [Fantasy Arena Builds](/en/builds) first.

### What should I spend code rewards on before Hard Mode?

Spend code rewards on the part of your setup that fixes the biggest weakness. That may be a class, wand, trait, or upgrade path depending on your current build.

### Is Hard Mode better for farming?

It can be better if your build clears it consistently. If Hard Mode causes repeated failed runs, improve your class, wand, traits, and boss prep before using it as a farming route.
