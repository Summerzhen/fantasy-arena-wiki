## Fantasy Arena Beginner Guide

Your first goal is not to chase the rarest roll. Build a reliable loop: survive early waves, improve the part of your setup that is limiting damage, and save enough Burgers for a meaningful class, wand, or trait upgrade.

## What to Upgrade First

Use this progression checklist after each run:

1. **Fix early wave clear.** If normal enemies survive too long, improve your wand or damage-focused class before buying a niche upgrade.
2. **Keep a reserve.** Do not spend every Burger on random rolls; keep enough for the next upgrade that completes your plan.
3. **Add survivability after damage is stable.** A little extra safety is valuable once you can clear the current wave comfortably.
4. **Test one change at a time.** A short run makes it easier to tell whether the class, wand, or trait actually helped.

For a current class recommendation, use the [Classes Guide](/en/classes) and [Class Tier List](/en/tier/fantasy-arena-class-tier-list). For free starting resources, check [Fantasy Arena Codes](/en/codes/fantasy-arena-codes).

## Best Starting Class

Choose the class that gives you a clear damage plan with the wand you already have. Mage-style progression is a practical beginner direction because it is easy to understand and connects naturally to wand upgrades, but a lower-rarity class with better synergy can be the stronger choice for your account.

| Situation | What to choose |
|-----------|----------------|
| Brand new player | A flexible, easy-to-use damage class |
| Playing solo | Reliable wave clear and enough safety to recover from mistakes |
| Playing co-op | A class that complements your team's damage or support gap |
| Chasing high waves | A class with scaling that still contributes after early upgrades |

## Best Early Wand

Upgrade the wand that improves your current bottleneck. If enemies are reaching you, prioritize damage consistency and range; if ordinary waves are easy but bosses stop your run, prioritize sustained boss damage. See [Best Wands in Fantasy Arena](/en/wands/fantasy-arena-best-wands) before replacing a wand only because it has a lower rarity label.

V1.4 also added wand stacking. Read the [Wand Stacking Guide](/en/wands/fantasy-arena-wand-stacking) once you have a stable base wand; do not sacrifice basic wave clear just to test a new interaction.

## How to Farm Faster

- Use the strongest run you can clear consistently rather than restarting for a perfect setup.
- Spend early rewards on the upgrade that lets you reach the next wave or reward breakpoint.
- Keep your character near a safe route so you can kite enemies while your wand continues dealing damage.
- In co-op, split roles: one player can focus on pressure while another covers dangerous spawns or revives.
- Recheck your build after each major update. The Frost update may change which classes, wands, and traits scale best.

## How to Survive More Waves

Do not stand in the middle of a crowd. Move around the edge of the arena, keep a retreat path open, and use terrain or corners to buy time while abilities recover. Save your strongest cooldown for an elite or boss wave instead of spending it on enemies you can clear safely.

## Boss Tips

Bosses are a test of uptime and positioning, not just burst damage. Keep moving, attack from the safest range your wand allows, and avoid letting ordinary enemies surround you while you focus the boss. If a boss forces you into a cramped position, reset your route early instead of waiting until you are already trapped.

## Common Beginner Mistakes

- Spending all Burgers before you know which part of the build is weak.
- Changing class, wand, and traits at the same time, then being unable to identify what improved.
- Treating a tier list as permanent after a major update.
- Entering a boss wave with no escape route or unused defensive option.
- Following an unverified code or guide that invents rewards, stats, or mechanics.

## FAQ

### What is the best way to farm in Fantasy Arena?

The best farming method is the highest run you can clear consistently. Improve early wave clear first, save enough resources for a meaningful upgrade, and only then push for a longer run.

### What should I max upgrade first?

Max the part of your setup that is directly stopping progress. For most beginners that means a reliable wand or class damage upgrade, not a rare effect that does not improve the current run.

### Should I play solo or co-op?

Solo is useful for learning enemy routes and testing builds. Co-op can be better when your group covers different roles and prevents a single weak point from ending the run.
