## Fantasy Arena Scythe

The official Roblox V1.4 notes list a **limited-time scythe** alongside a limited-time Mythic class. The listing does not yet provide the Scythe's exact stats, cost, skill names, or unlock route, so those details should be confirmed from the live game rather than guessed.

## How to Test the Scythe

1. Check the Frost event interface for the displayed unlock requirement.
2. Compare its live stats with your current wand and class.
3. Run the same early waves with and without the Scythe.
4. Record boss damage, survivability, and the number of upgrades needed to clear.

The best weapon is the one that improves your complete setup. Use the [Best Wands Guide](/en/wands/fantasy-arena-best-wands), [Class Tier List](/en/tier/fantasy-arena-class-tier-list), and [Frost Event Guide](/en/guide/fantasy-arena-frost-event) when comparing it.
