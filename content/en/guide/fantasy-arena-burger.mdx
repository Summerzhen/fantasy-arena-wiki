## Pro Tips to Maximize Your Burger Farming

Beyond the core farming method and code redemptions, these additional strategies will help you optimize every minute you spend earning burgers.

### 1. Always Run Hard Mode

The difference in burger payouts between normal and hard mode is substantial. Once you've unlocked the Mage class and Polished Wand, there's no reason to farm on normal. The turret strategy handles hard mode comfortably, and the increased rewards compound over time.

### 2. Don't Skip Auto Skip

Enabling the Auto Skip feature eliminates downtime between waves. Over the course of dozens of runs, those saved seconds add up to extra runs per session and significantly more burgers earned per hour.

### 3. Reset at Wave 26, Not Later

Community reports indicate that pushing past wave 26 yields diminishing returns on time invested. The burger-per-minute ratio peaks around wave 25–26, making the reset at that point the most efficient choice for sustained farming.
