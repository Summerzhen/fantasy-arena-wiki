## FAQ

### How do I get Fantasy Arena burgers fast as a complete beginner?

Start by checking the newest promo codes in the lobby, then switch to the Mage class, equip the Polished Wand, and run Solo Hard matches with Auto Skip enabled. Push to Wave 26 and reset—each loop can pay 125 to 150 burgers in about four minutes, depending on the run.

### Do burgers spawn on the map during gameplay?

Yes. Burgers randomly appear around the map while you play through waves. Walk over them to collect them. They're a nice bonus, but they shouldn't distract you from upgrading Pylons and your wand. Community reports suggest spawns happen more frequently in open areas near the grassy platform.

### What happens if I don't reset at Wave 26?

You can keep playing past Wave 26, but the difficulty ramps up significantly and the burger payout per additional wave diminishes relative to the time invested. Resetting at Wave 26 maximizes your burgers-per-minute ratio, which is the core principle behind getting Fantasy Arena burgers fast. Repeating the short loop is more efficient than pushing deep into later waves with a high risk of failure.
