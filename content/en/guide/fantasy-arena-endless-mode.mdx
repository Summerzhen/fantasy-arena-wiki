## What Is Endless Mode in Fantasy Arena?

Endless Waves is the core Fantasy Arena mode. Enemies become harder as a run continues, so success depends on a build that can keep its damage, control, and survivability online over time.

The most useful mental model is:

**Classes + Wands + Traits + Summons = Build → Higher Waves**

There is no single universal answer for the highest wave. A setup that clears normal enemies quickly may struggle against a boss, while a defensive setup may survive longer but earn rewards more slowly.

## A Simple Endless Run Plan

### Early waves: establish the build

Use the opening waves to confirm that your class and wand work together. Prioritize the first upgrade that improves consistent wave clear, then add traits that support the same damage or survival plan.

### Mid waves: protect your win condition

As enemy pressure rises, stop spreading resources across unrelated bonuses. Keep your main damage source active, preserve defensive cooldowns for dangerous waves, and use your summon when it solves a specific problem rather than on cooldown without a plan.

### Boss waves: change your priority

Bosses test single-target damage, movement, and resource management. Leave enough room to dodge, keep your strongest defensive option available, and review the [Fantasy Arena Bosses](/en/bosses) guides before farming a known encounter.

## Endless Build Checklist

| Question | What to verify |
|---|---|
| Class | Does its ability support the intended role? |
| Wand | Does it cover the class's range, damage, or control gap? |
| Traits | Do the bonuses reinforce one plan instead of competing plans? |
| Summon | Is its effect confirmed and useful in the target wave range? |
| Boss plan | Can the build handle a pressure spike without exhausting resources? |
| Reset point | Is continuing the run better than starting a fresh run? |

## Common Endless Mode Mistakes

- Building for early-wave speed while ignoring boss damage.
- Changing class, wand, and traits at the same time, making the result impossible to compare.
- Spending every defensive ability before a known pressure spike.
- Copying an unverified wave record or drop rate as if it were an official mechanic.
- Ranking a summon before testing its actual effect in a repeatable run.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best class for Endless Waves?

It depends on the role and the rest of the build. Compare a class with the intended wand, traits, and summon instead of ranking it in isolation.

### What should I upgrade first?

Upgrade the part of the build that most reliably fixes your current failure: wave clear, survivability, or boss damage. Keep the rest of the setup consistent while testing.

### Is there a confirmed highest wave?

This wiki does not treat an unverified player claim as an official record. Add a record only with a date, version, mode, build, and evidence.
