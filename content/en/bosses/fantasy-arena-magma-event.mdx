### The Basic Monarch (Wave 25)

The Basic Monarch is the most common boss you'll face at wave 25. According to player experience, this boss has a straightforward attack pattern but comes with one tricky mechanic: it splits into two when damaged enough.

**Key tactics:**
- Activate your defensive stance skill before engaging
- Focus damage on one clone at a time
- Retreat to your base/cliff position if overwhelmed
- The split clones share the original's low speed—kite them easily

The Enchanted Guardian class shines here. Its defensive skill makes you nearly unkillable while active, and the passive bonuses (10% extra speed, health, and regeneration) give you the sustain needed to outlast the boss.

### The Skeleton (Wave 25 Alternative)

Less commonly encountered at wave 25, the Skeleton boss is reportedly simpler to defeat than the Basic Monarch. It doesn't split and has predictable attack patterns. Treat it as a free shard opportunity.

### The Fastest Monarch (Wave 50)

This is the prize fight of the Fantasy Arena magma event. The Fastest Monarch is the most valuable boss because she always arrives accompanied by shard holders—enemies that drop significant amounts of Magma Shards when defeated.

**Why she's the best boss for farming:**
- Guaranteed shard holder escorts
- High total shard payout per encounter
- Manageable damage if you have Firewall upgrades
- Defensive stance negates most of her threat

The strategy is simple: when the Fastest Monarch appears, pop your defensive skill, focus on the shard holders first to collect their drops, then finish off the boss. Player experience indicates you can collect hundreds of shards from this single encounter.

### The Wave 50 Reset Strategy

Here's the critical insight most players miss: shard spawn rates drop significantly after wave 50. While shards still appear, they don't spawn nearly as frequently as in the first 50 waves. This means continuing past wave 50 is inefficient for shard farming.

**Optimal approach:** After defeating the Fastest Monarch at wave 50, respawn and start a new game. You'll return to the high-spawn-rate early waves and accumulate shards much faster than slogging through waves 51+.
