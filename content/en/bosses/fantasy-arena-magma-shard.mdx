## Understanding Shard Spawn Mechanics and Wave Timing

Not all waves are created equal when it comes to shard drops. According to player experience, the first 50 waves are where the real money is. After wave 50, shard spawns drop off significantly, making extended runs far less efficient per minute invested.

| Wave Range | Shard Spawn Rate | Recommended Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1–24 | Moderate | AFK farm, upgrade wand |
| 25 | High (Boss wave) | Collect shards from boss fight |
| 26–49 | Moderate to High | Continue AFK farming |
| 50 | Very High (Boss + shard holders) | Full engagement, collect everything |
| 51+ | Low | Respawn and start a new run |

The critical takeaway: don't linger past wave 50. Restarting a fresh run on hard mode is dramatically more efficient than slogging through diminished spawn rates in later waves.

## Boss Encounters and Shard Collection

Bosses are your primary shard-collection windows. While regular waves passively generate currency, boss fights concentrate shards into a single, lootable moment—especially when shard holders accompany the boss.

### Wave 25 Boss: Basic Monarch or Skeleton

The first boss appears at wave 25, and it's typically one of two options:

- **Basic Monarch**: Splits into two forms during the fight but goes down quickly with sustained fire
- **Skeleton**: Straightforward damage sponge with no major gimmicks

Neither poses a serious threat to an Enchanted Guardian with a leveled fire wand. Activate your defensive stance skill, hold your ground, and collect any shards that drop.

### Wave 50 Boss: The Fastest Monarch

This is the run's highlight. The Fastest Monarch arrives at wave 50 and consistently brings shard holders with her. Player experience describes seeing massive clusters of shards on the ground during this encounter. The Enchanted Guardian's defensive skill makes her damage negligible, allowing you to focus entirely on collection.

| Boss | Wave | Shard Potential | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic Monarch | 25 | Moderate | Low |
| Skeleton | 25 | Moderate | Low |
| Fastest Monarch | 50 | Very High | Moderate |

After defeating the Fastest Monarch and vacuuming up her shard haul, immediately respawn. The diminishing returns beyond wave 50 make restarting the clear optimal choice.

The Enchanted Guardian class offers unparalleled survivability through its defensive skill, which grants near-invulnerability when activated. The passive bonuses seal the deal:

- **+10% Speed**: Reposition quickly during boss fights
- **+10% Health**: Larger buffer against burst damage

The fire wand's rapid-fire mechanic is the cornerstone of the AFK strategy. It creates a wall of projectiles that enemies can't walk through. Community reports suggest other wands may work, but the fire wand's combination of speed and stagger is unmatched for this specific farming method.

| Wand Type | Rapid Fire | Stagger Potential | AFK Viability |

| Lightning Wand | High | Low | Fair |

If you're experimenting with other wands, prioritize attack speed and crowd control over raw damage per hit.
