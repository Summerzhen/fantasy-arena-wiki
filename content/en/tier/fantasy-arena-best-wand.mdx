- Rolling for a rare wand without a class plan.

- Treating damage as the only useful wand stat.
- Forgetting that traits can change wand value.
- Following a wand ranking without checking your own build goal.

## Best Wand FAQ

### What is the best wand in Fantasy Arena?

The best wand depends on your class, traits, and goal. Phoenix-style sustain and Fire-style damage are useful examples, but your build context matters more than a single name.

### Should I spend code rewards on wand rolls?

Only if a better wand is the main thing holding your build back. If your class or traits are weak, those rewards may be better spent elsewhere.
