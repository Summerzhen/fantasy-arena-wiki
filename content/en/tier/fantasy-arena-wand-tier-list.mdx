## Wand Tier List FAQ

### What is the best wand in Fantasy Arena?

Phoenix-style sustain and strong damage wands are the safest top directions, but the best wand depends on your class, traits, and build goal.

### Should I roll for wands before choosing a class?

If you already have rewards saved, choose a class direction first when possible. If you roll a strong wand early, build around that wand with a matching class and traits.

### Can a lower-tier wand still be useful?

Yes. A lower-rarity wand with good synergy can outperform a rare wand that does not fit your class or traits.
