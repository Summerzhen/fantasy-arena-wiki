## Fantasy Arena Trait Tier List Overview

This Fantasy Arena Trait Tier List ranks trait directions by how consistently they improve a real build. The safest traits are not always the rarest ones. The best traits are the ones that make your class and wand work together.

For the full trait hub, read [Fantasy Arena Traits](/en/traits). For practical trait choices, use [Fantasy Arena Best Traits](/en/traits/fantasy-arena-best-traits).

## Trait Tier List

| Tier | Trait Direction | Best Use | Why It Ranks Here |
|------|-----------------|----------|-------------------|
| S | Damage scaling | Wave clear, bosses, high waves | Strong damage is useful in almost every run. |
| S | Cooldown or uptime | Mage-style, wand-heavy, summon-style setups | More uptime makes the rest of the build more reliable. |
| A | Survivability | Hard Mode, solo play, boss pressure | Keeps strong builds alive when damage alone is not enough. |
| A | Economy | Early game and farming | Speeds up progression before your setup is complete. |
| B | Mobility | Dodging pressure and repositioning | Useful, but usually weaker than damage or survival. |
| B | Narrow utility | Specific class or wand problems | Valuable only when it solves a clear weakness. |

## S Tier Traits

S-tier trait directions improve the widest range of Fantasy Arena builds. Damage scaling and uptime are strong because every class and wand benefits when enemies die faster or key effects are available more often.

Use S-tier traits when:

- You are pushing higher waves.
- Your wand already fits your class.
- Bosses live too long.
- Your build has enough survival and needs more output.

## A Tier Traits

A-tier traits are excellent when your current goal matches them. Survivability matters more in [Hard Mode](/en/guide/fantasy-arena-hard-mode), bosses, and solo play. Economy traits are best when your account still needs burgers or upgrade resources.

Do not keep economy traits forever just because they helped early. Once your build starts failing in combat, shift toward damage, uptime, or survival.

## B Tier Traits

B-tier traits are not bad, but they need a reason. Mobility, range, or narrow utility can be useful if your build has one specific problem. If you cannot explain what the trait fixes, it is usually worse than a more direct damage or survival trait.

## Fantasy Arena Trait Tier List FAQ

### What is the best trait in Fantasy Arena?

The best trait depends on your class and wand, but damage scaling and cooldown or uptime traits are usually the safest top-tier directions.

### Are economy traits S tier?

Economy traits are excellent early, but they are not always S tier for combat. Use them while progression speed matters, then swap toward combat value when waves or bosses become the problem.

### Should I follow the trait tier list exactly?

Use it as a decision framework. If your build already has enough damage but dies in Hard Mode, an A-tier survival trait may be better than another S-tier damage trait.
