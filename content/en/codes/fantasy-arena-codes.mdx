## Fantasy Arena Strategy Guide: Making the Most of Your Free Burgers

Getting free burgers from Fantasy Arena codes is only half the battle. Spending them wisely is what separates top players from the rest. Based on player experience, here's a prioritized spending strategy for early and mid-game runs.

### Early Game (Waves 1-10)

Your first priority should be upgrading your starting wand and picking up a second wand as soon as possible. Area damage is especially helpful for clearing grouped enemies, so a strong wand roll can matter more than a small health upgrade at the start.

| Priority | Upgrade | Impact |
|----------|---------|--------|
| 1 | Second Wand | Doubles your damage output |
| 2 | Wand Upgrade | Improves base damage per hit |
| 3 | Mage Level | Scales your overall magical power |

### Mid Game (Waves 10-20)

This is where pylons become essential. Pylons act as automated turrets that attack enemies for you, and they are among the most cost-effective upgrades once enemies begin arriving in larger groups.

- Stack enemies together before using your mana explosion for maximum efficiency.
- Upgrade pylons before wave 15 if your damage starts falling behind.
- Eat burgers that drop on the ground during waves; they restore HP and keep you alive.
- Save burst abilities for grouped tank enemies, not scattered weak enemies.

### Late Game (Waves 20+)

Past wave 20, the difficulty ramps up. Dark fairies, tank enemies, and boss-type encounters punish weak builds, so every code reward should support a focused class, wand, and trait setup.

| Wave Range | Primary Threat | Recommended Counter |
