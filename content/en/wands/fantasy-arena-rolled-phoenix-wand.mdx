## How Crate Rolling Works in the New Update

Fantasy Arena's latest update introduced a limited-time crate system featuring several powerful wands. Community reports often favor hard mode for farming, but your best route should be based on rewards per minute rather than a fixed payout claim.

### Tips for Maximizing Your Roll Efficiency

- **Test hard mode before committing.** If hard mode slows your clear or causes deaths, normal mode may produce better rewards per minute.
- **Target event crystals.** These start spawning around wave 12 and can also drop from shiny mobs—those glowing enemies that appear randomly.
- **Save your shards.** Shards are harder to come by than bricks, so spend them strategically on the crate you want most.
- **Don't skip waves.** Even though bosses don't spawn after wave 125, continuing to wave 300 still generates solid currency for additional rolls.
