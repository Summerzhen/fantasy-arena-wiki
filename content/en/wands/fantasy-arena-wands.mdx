## How to Roll Fantasy Arena Wands from Crates

Obtaining Fantasy Arena wands revolves around the game's crate system. Each wand type corresponds to a specific crate, and these crates are available for a limited time—meaning you'll need to act fast before they rotate out.

### Crate Rolling Strategy

| Strategy | Description | Recommended For |
|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| Single Rolls | Roll one crate at a time for controlled spending | F2P players, cautious spenders |
| Bulk Rolls | Open multiple crates in succession | Players with large shard reserves |
| Save and Wait | Hoard currency until desired crate appears | Patient players targeting specific wands |
| Hard Mode Farming | Grind hard mode for maximum currency | Endgame players with strong builds |

The key currency for crate rolls comes from several sources:

- **Bricks**: Earned from completing waves, with massive payouts at higher wave counts (community reports indicate 67,000+ bricks from extended runs)
- **Shards**: Secondary currency earned alongside bricks
- **Event Crystals**: New currency type that begins spawning at wave 12, dropped by shiny mobs and found as world pickups

### Hard Mode: Your Ticket to More Rolls

Hard mode was added alongside the wand update, and it's the most efficient way to stockpile currency for crate rolling. Players report that hard mode yields significantly more bricks and shards per run compared to normal mode. If you're serious about collecting Fantasy Arena wands, mastering hard mode should be your first priority.

## Fantasy Arena Wands FAQ

### What is the rarest wand in Fantasy Arena?

The Phoenix Wand is currently the rarest of all Fantasy Arena wands, classified as Mythic rarity from a limited-time crate. Its unique revival ability and limited availability make it the most difficult wand to obtain.

### Can Fantasy Arena wands be traded between players?

Based on current community reports, Fantasy Arena wands appear to be bound to the player who rolls them. There's no confirmed trading system in place as of the latest update.

### Do Fantasy Arena wands work with all classes?

Yes, Fantasy Arena wands can be equipped regardless of your class choice. However, certain wands synergize much better with specific classes—the Phoenix Wand pairs exceptionally well with the Druid's healing abilities, while the Midas Wand complements economy-focused Commander builds.

### How long do limited crates stay available?

The exact duration of limited crates hasn't been officially confirmed by the developers. Community experience suggests they rotate out after a few weeks, so it's best to roll for your desired wand as early as possible rather than risking the crate disappearing.

### Is the Midas Wand worth rolling for?

If the community speculation about gold transmutation is accurate, the Midas Wand could be the best choice for players focused on currency farming. Hard mode already provides excellent brick and shard payouts, and a wand that further boosts income would dramatically accelerate your ability to roll for other items.
