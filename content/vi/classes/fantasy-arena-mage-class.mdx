## Fantasy Arena Mage Class

Mage la mot huong class de lap ke hoach vi no ro vai tro: damage on dinh, dung wand hop ly va traits ho tro uptime hoac damage.

## Mage Build Core

| Traits | Damage, cooldown, uptime hoac sinh ton |
