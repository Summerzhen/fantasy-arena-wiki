## Fantasy Arena Hard Mode

Hard Mode danh cho nguoi choi da co build on dinh o normal. Truoc khi vao, hay kiem tra codes, class, wand va traits. Neu mot phan qua yeu, Hard Mode se lam loi do ro hon.

| Codes | Nhan [Fantasy Arena Codes](/vi/codes/fantasy-arena-codes) truoc khi tieu tai nguyen. |
| Class | Dung class co vai tro ro trong [Classes](/vi/classes). |
| Wand | Chon wand co damage on dinh trong [Wands](/vi/wands). |
| Traits | Uu tien damage, uptime hoac sinh ton trong [Traits](/vi/traits). |

## Khi Nao Nen Vao

Hay vao Hard Mode khi ban da clear waves normal kha on dinh. Neu thua vi thieu damage, nang wand hoac traits. Neu thua vi ap luc, them sinh ton truoc.

## Loi Thuong Gap

- Vao chi vi Hard Mode co reward cao hon.
- Quen nhan codes truoc khi tieu tai nguyen.
- Dung farming build nhung thieu phong thu.
- Doi class nhung khong doi wand va traits.

## FAQ

### Hard Mode co dang choi khong?

Co, neu build da on dinh. Neu normal van kho, hay nang class, wand va traits truoc.

### Nen dung build nao cho Hard Mode?

Bat dau voi [Fantasy Arena Best Builds](/vi/builds/fantasy-arena-best-builds), roi chinh theo van de: damage, sinh ton hoac boss pressure.
