## FAQ

### Wand nao tot nhat?

Phoenix-style sustain va wands dano on dinh la huong an toan, nhung dap an phu thuoc class, traits va muc tieu.

### Co nen roll wand truoc khi chon class?

Neu co the plan, chon class truoc. Neu da co wand tot, build quanh wand do.

### Wand tier thap co dung duoc khong?

Co. Wand it hiem hon nhung synergy tot co the hon wand hiem khong hop build.
